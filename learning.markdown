This page tracks what I am reading and studying. It gets updated as I progress.

---

**Designing Data-Intensive Applications** — Martin Kleppmann *(started May 2026)*
A deep dive into the foundations of large-scale data systems: storage engines, replication, 
partitioning, and distributed transactions. Essential reading for anyone who wants to build 
systems that actually hold up in production.

---

**Fluent Python** — Luciano Ramalho *(started June 2026)*
Going beyond writing Python that works, towards writing Python that is clean, idiomatic, 
and efficient. Particularly interesting on data structures, decorators, and the Python 
data model.

---

**Designing Machine Learning Systems** — Chip Huyen *(started June 2026)*
Bridges the gap between building models and deploying systems that work reliably in the 
real world. Covers the full ML lifecycle from data collection to monitoring in production.
